# molthub GEO Diagnosis

Diagnosis date: 2026-08-17

## Primary bottleneck

**molthub had a credible sales proposition but not yet a complete, independently inspectable evidence system.**

The homepage explained what molthub sells, but a skeptical buyer or retrieval system could not follow a clear path from brand definition to methodology, dated experiment, measurement limits and maintained results.

## Why this matters most

Adding more generic GEO articles would not resolve the core trust question:

> What has molthub measured, how was it measured, what changed, and what remains unknown?

Without those answers, the site risks looking like a service landing page that discusses GEO rather than a practitioner that documents its own process.

## Evidence

- No dedicated `/about` entity page existed.
- No dedicated `/methodology` page explained inputs, actions, outputs, measurements and limits.
- No dated public self-experiment existed.
- Report visuals used static numbers without a visible sample-data label on every data view.
- The case library contained no permissioned public client result.
- The Insights page promised daily publishing without an automated or maintained schedule.
- The desktop navigation contained nine items and duplicated the Account destination.

## Secondary bottlenecks

### P0 — Truth and trust

- Label every illustrative metric as sample data.
- Remove publication-frequency claims that the site cannot maintain.
- Distinguish the installable web app from a native Windows application.
- Keep private and transactional routes out of search results.

### P0 — Entity and hierarchy

- Lock the public brand name to `molthub`.
- Publish a dedicated About page with extractable facts.
- Publish the operating methodology and its limitations.
- Connect service, methodology, research, insights and conversion through descriptive links.

### P1 — Performance and media

- Replace full-size PNG article covers with compressed WebP assets.
- Add native lazy loading and asynchronous decoding.
- Avoid repeating the hero poster as a substitute for evidence throughout the homepage.

### P1 — Measurement

- Create a bounded buyer-query set.
- Store date, platform, query, mention, citation, factual accuracy and competitor observations.
- Treat unmeasured fields as unknown.

### P2 — Authority

- Publish useful first-party experiments.
- Seek legitimate ecosystem listings, technical collaborations and independent references.
- Do not buy links, fabricate reviews or manufacture community mentions.

## Priority decision

The first implementation should improve truth, entity clarity and inspectable methodology before creating additional topic pages. New content is valuable only when it closes a documented query or evidence gap.
