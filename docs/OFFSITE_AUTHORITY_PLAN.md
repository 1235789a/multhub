# molthub Off-site Authority Plan

Version: 2026-08-17

## Principle

The molthub website can define its own service, method and evidence. It cannot independently prove that outside communities trust or recommend the brand.

## Priority channels

| Channel | Legitimate asset | Verification | First action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GitHub | Public measurement template, methodology notes or small audit utilities | Public repository and commit history | Publish the monitoring schema after it is used internally |
| Web3 ecosystem directories | Accurate company/service profile | Public listing and consistent official links | Identify directories relevant to stablecoin payments and developer tooling |
| Founder/operator communities | Useful public audit or answer to a real question | Discussion remains public and attributable | Share one evidence-led observation, not a sales pitch |
| Partner websites | Integration or joint research page | Both organizations publish matching facts | Start only after a real collaboration exists |
| Independent reviews | Honest service review from a real buyer | Reviewer identity and scope are documented | Ask only after delivery; never script the conclusion |
| Guest research | Original Web3 AI-visibility analysis | Named author, methodology and sources | Pitch a small data-backed study, not a generic GEO article |
| Social profiles | Consistent brand description and research updates | Same official domain and entity facts | Lock the same one-sentence definition across profiles |

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Purchased bulk backlinks
- Fake Reddit or forum discussions
- Fabricated directory awards
- Automated community spam
- False Wikipedia or Wikidata claims
- Unverified client logos
- Reciprocal pages created only to manipulate ranking or AI answers

## First external asset

After the self-GEO experiment has at least four dated observations, publish the method and anonymized data as a small public repository. The asset should be useful even to someone who never buys a molthub service.
