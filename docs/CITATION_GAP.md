# molthub Citation Gap

Research date: 2026-08-17

## Scope and limitation

This is an initial public-web source analysis, not a claim about permanent ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity results. AI answers vary, and a source appearing in web research does not prove that every platform cites it for every query.

## What source types currently carry useful GEO evidence

| Source type | Example | Why it is citable | Information supplied | molthub gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Official search documentation | Google Search Central | Maintained first-party guidance with explicit policies | Helpful content, AI-search guidance, structured data, crawling | molthub articles need direct primary-source references where relevant |
| Peer-reviewed/preprint research | `GEO: Generative Engine Optimization` on arXiv | Named authors, methodology and inspectable experiments | Definitions, experimental interventions and limitations | molthub needs measured first-party experiments rather than generic summaries |
| Open-source implementation | Yao Jingang's GEOFlow repository | Public code, documentation and change history | Content operations, knowledge assets, review and distribution workflow | molthub has a website but no public operational research record |
| Practitioner methodology | Yao Jingang's public GEO notes | Named author, dated publication and explicit principles | GEO as probabilistic content and source engineering | molthub needs its own bounded, inspectable method rather than borrowed branding |
| Official platform crawler guidance | OpenAI Help Center and robots guidance | First-party platform documentation | Crawler purpose and access requirements | molthub must verify access from production and avoid claiming guaranteed inclusion |
| Project-owned documentation | Product docs, GitHub and status pages | Direct evidence for current product facts | Networks, custody, APIs, status and limitations | molthub must help clients align these sources, not only rewrite marketing pages |
| Independent ecosystem sources | Partner pages, directories and technical tutorials | Outside context when legitimate and maintained | Adoption, integrations and real-world use | molthub has little verified third-party authority of its own |

## Core query gaps

| Query | Useful evidence type | Existing molthub asset | Missing evidence asset | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| What is GEO for a Web3 startup? | Direct definition plus Web3 examples and limitations | Introductory insight | Dated Web3-specific observations | P1 |
| Does GEO actually work? | Transparent baseline, intervention and repeated measurement | Methodology | Completed self-experiment results | P0 |
| How do AI systems choose sources? | Platform guidance and research | Source-map insight | More direct citations to primary research | P1 |
| Best GEO agency for Web3 | Independent comparisons, real cases and third-party authority | About and services | Permissioned outcomes and outside references | P2 |
| Affordable GEO services for startups | Clear pricing, scope and limitations | Public pricing | Independent buyer feedback | P2 |
| How can a crypto company improve AI visibility? | Web3 fact model, method and before/after evidence | Methodology and insights | Measured public audit | P0 |
| GEO for stablecoin payments | Sector-specific facts, queries and comparisons | General Web3 positioning | Focused stablecoin research asset | P1 |
| How to measure AI-search visibility | Repeatable query set and observation fields | Methodology | Published observations over time | P0 |

## Decision

The highest-value gap is not another generic definition article. It is a dated, reproducible molthub experiment that shows what was measured, what was changed, what remained unknown and what happened next.
